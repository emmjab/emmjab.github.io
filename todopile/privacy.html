Privacy Policy

Todopile for iPhone and Android

Short version

Todopile doesn't collect or transmit any personal data. There's no account, no analytics, and no advertising in the app. Your tasks, categories, and settings are stored only on your own device (on iPhone, using Apple's on-device SwiftData framework; on Android, in the app's private storage) and are never sent anywhere.

What data does the app store?

The tasks you add (their titles, sizes, categories, whether they're to do, done, or won't do, and when you added or finished them), your repeating tasks (their titles and when they repeat), the categories you create (names and colors), and your settings (theme, view, shelf sort order, and pomodoro length). This is stored locally on your device only, for the sole purpose of showing you your own to-do list.

What data leaves your device?

Nothing. The app makes no network requests at all, and does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or any third-party SDK. It works fully offline.

Like other app data, Todopile's data is included in your device's own backups if you have those turned on: iCloud Backup or a computer backup on iPhone, or your Google account's device backup on Android. Those backups are handled by Apple or Google under your account; the developer has no access to them.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the pomodoro timer schedules a notification on your device for when time's up, and a repeating task set to notify you schedules one for when its book goes on your pile. They're created and delivered entirely on your device; no server is involved. A notification can show a task's title, so it may be visible on your lock screen.

On Android, the app can also ask for the "Alarms & reminders" permission so these notifications arrive on time, and it sets them up again after your phone restarts. Neither involves any of your data leaving the device.

How do I delete this data?

Tap the ··· menu and choose Erase all data to delete every task and category and reset all settings. You can also delete a single task from its edit screen, or delete the app, which removes all of its data from your device. Since nothing is stored anywhere else, deletion is immediate and complete (apart from any device backups you've made yourself).

Children's privacy

Since the app collects no personal data from anyone, including children, there is nothing to disclose or protect beyond what's already described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes (for example, if a future version of the app adds syncing between devices), this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date below will change accordingly.
