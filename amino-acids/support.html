Amino Acids — Support

Flashcards, Quiz, 3D & History

How do I reset my progress?

Open the Progress tab and use the reset option there. This clears quiz history and reviewed-amino-acid tracking stored on your device; it doesn't affect anything outside the app.

Is my data private?

Yes. Amino Acids doesn't have accounts and doesn't collect or transmit any data. Everything — quiz scores, review history — stays on your device. (A few flashcards link out to external scientific publications, which is the only time the app ever opens the network, and only when you tap one.) See the privacy policy for details.

I found a bug, or have a suggestion

Email e.jablonski@gmail.com with what you were doing and what happened. Screenshots help a lot.

Where does the discovery history data come from?

Every amino acid's discovery story (who found it, when, and from what source) was individually checked against primary publications rather than a summary source. Each entry includes a citation, shown on the back of its flashcard and in the Discovery History list.
